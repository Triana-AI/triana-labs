# Triana design-partner research — 20 net-new qualified companies

Research date: 2026-08-03. Exclusion set: all companies in the outreach Google Sheet (~80, including renamed forms — Leya→Legora caught). Method: 6 parallel research passes (CX, healthcare, recruiting/vertical SaaS, fintech/insurance, legal, commerce/logistics) over company sites, Ashby/Greenhouse job APIs, press, engineering blogs.

## Executive summary

**Three strongest clusters**

1. **Regulated financial conversation agents** (interface.ai, Prodigal, Liberate, Casca, Skit.ai, Gradient Labs) — most explicit release-verification language found anywhere: interface.ai PM req demands "offline golden sets, regression testing, hallucination measurement… canaries, rollback"; Prodigal hiring engineers to build "evaluation framework… conversation quality, empathy, and compliance adherence." Compliance turns behavioral regression into a regulatory event.
2. **Healthcare patient-access voice agents** (Ellipsis Health, Assort Health, Hyro, Hippocratic) — Ellipsis hiring an "AI Evaluations Engineer" + 3 SDETs to hand-build a voice-agent testing platform; Assort's "Agent Operations" role must build agent-QA frameworks mid-hypergrowth.
3. **Consequential-action commerce/CX agents** (Siena, Gorgias, Yuma, Vooma, HappyRobot, Zuma) — agents issue refunds, book loads, collect rent. Siena's CEO published the roadmap gap himself ("We're building automated regression suites" — future tense); Yuma's release process is a 5% live-traffic canary with humans reviewing every response.

**Most common pain evidence:** job postings asking a new hire to build eval/regression infrastructure from *real production conversations* (interface.ai, Prodigal, Eve, Gradient Labs, Siena, Gorgias, Ellipsis, Zuma, Assort); second: release safety via live-traffic canaries plus manual transcript review (Yuma, Casca, Avoca's TAM model).

**ICP verdict: strengthened, one sharpening.** Series A–C teams operating customer-facing agents demonstrably feel this pain — hiring for it, blogging about it, running manual canaries. Sharpening: the largest agent vendors build it in-house (Zowie "Tester" deploy gate; Parloa Simulations; Intercom Fin batch testing). Window = mid-stage vendors *before* they staff that team; differentiation = production-prefix replay fidelity vs. generated scenarios.

**Contact first:** Gradient Labs, interface.ai, Zuma, Ellipsis Health, Siena AI (explicit pain + founder-level accessibility).
